Open **Settings** in the navigation, or from the account menu. The page is titled **Settings** and covers **Your
account, sessions and servers.**

## Account

**Account** shows your **Email** and since when you have been a member (**Member since**).

## Change password

Fill in **Current password**, **New password** and **Confirm new password**, then choose **Change password**.
The new password needs at least 10 characters (at most 256).

<Callout tone="info">
  Changing it signs out every other session of your account. The browser you use stays signed in.
</Callout>

There is no password reset by email yet. Keep your password in a password manager.

## Sessions

**Signed in on a shared or lost device? End those sessions here.**

- **Sign out other sessions** asks **Sign out other sessions?** and, after **Sign out others**, signs out every
  other browser. This session stays signed in.
- **Sign out here** signs out the browser you are using.

You can also sign out from the account menu at the bottom of the navigation, with **Sign out**.

## Server access

Lists every server paired with your account, with its status in words and the date it was paired, such as
**Paired** followed by the date. **Remove server** opens the same confirmation as on the server page; see
[Rename and remove](/servers/rename-and-remove#remove-a-server). Removing a server revokes its bridge,
disconnects it and removes it from ComfiConf.

With no servers paired, the section says **No servers are paired.** and links to **Pair a server**.

## Appearance

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. **System** follows your device's setting and is the default. The choice
is **Stored in this browser.**, so each browser keeps its own. The same choice is in the **Color theme** menu next
to your account.
