---
title: Create an account
description: Sign up at app.comficonf.com with an email address and a password.
---

Your ComfiConf account owns your servers. Create it at
[app.comficonf.com/register](https://app.comficonf.com/register).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to [app.comficonf.com](https://app.comficonf.com) and choose **Create an account**. The page is titled
    **Create your account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm password**. The password needs at least 10 characters (at most
    256).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    Choose **Create account**. You are signed in and land on **Your servers**, which is empty until you pair a
    server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout tone="warning" title="Keep your password in a password manager">
  There is no password reset by email yet. If you forget your password, you cannot recover the account
  yourself.
</Callout>

To sign in later, open app.comficonf.com and use **Sign in** on the **Welcome back** page. You can change your
password and sign out other sessions on the [Settings](/settings) page.
